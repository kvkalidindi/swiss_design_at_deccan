05 / Layout grid

12-column grid with a 24px gutter. Asymmetric layouts favour a 7+5 or 8+4 split, leaving generous left rails for headings and metadata — a Swiss-style nod to Müller-Brockmann.

Principle

One accent does the heavy lifting.

Blue-500 carries primary identity. Green is reserved for affirmation, growth, and chemistry signals. Status colors (success, warning, error, info) are intentionally not in this brand palette — they live in a separate functional palette to keep brand and signal cleanly separated.
